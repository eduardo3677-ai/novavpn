# EchoSmart visual assets

- Primary mark: cyan location/shield silhouette, night center and violet encrypted core.
- Dark background: `#060A12`.
- Cyan: `#54E8FF`; violet: `#8B5CF6`; mint/status: `#6EE7B7`.
- Keep one mark-width of clear space around the logo.
- Use `echo_notification.xml` for Android status notifications because notification icons must be monochrome.
- Use adaptive `@mipmap/ic_launcher` for launcher surfaces and `echo_launcher_monochrome.xml` for themed icons.
- Product navigation and protocol symbols use the `echo_nav_*`, `echo_protocol_*`, and `echo_profile_*` resource families.
